The replacement line (points per game)

For me = only games he started for you. Season / Last 30 / Last 14 = every game he played. +/- PPG = points per game above or below the replacement line for that row's position. +/- Pts = +/- PPG times games played. Notes: Replace? = Last 30 more than pts under the line. Poor performer = Last 30 PPG under the line. Small sample = fewer than starts for you. Color scales run against the largest value in each column of the rows shown.

★ = counts toward the replacement line. "Upgrade over" = your weakest active player at this position over the same window, and how many PPG this free agent would add.
